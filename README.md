Redora is a modern, AI-driven educational application designed to supercharge your study workflow. By leveraging lightning-fast LLM inference (powered by Groq), Redora allows students and lifelong learners to upload reading materials, notes, or PDFs, and instantly converts them into digestible summaries. It goes a step further by generating interactive multiple-choice practice quizzes and providing a chat interface to ask questions directly against the context of your uploaded documents. Features include secure Google authentication, PDF exports, and a unified history dashboard to track your learning progress.
